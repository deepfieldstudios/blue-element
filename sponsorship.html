Freediving is one of the fastest-growing aquatic sports on earth — and its biggest moments now play out live to a global audience, with broadcast-quality DiveEye footage carrying every dive from the surface to the bottom plate.

Blue Element Athletes are a tight, elite squad of national record holders and world-ranked competitors from three countries. Through 2026 they line up at the sport's marquee events — from AIDA and CMAS world championships to Vertical Blue and our own competition in Dominica.

A partnership puts your brand on the wetsuit, on the platform and in the content — alongside athletes the freediving world is watching go deep.
