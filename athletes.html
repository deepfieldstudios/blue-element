Depth Freediving

Arron Walker

United Kingdom · Depth to 90m · Targeting 100m

A lifetime on and around the ocean — yacht crew, scuba and freedive instructor — Arron began freediving in 2020 and has since reached 90m. This season he's chasing 100m across FIM, CWTB and CWT. Away from the line he's a committed ocean-conservation volunteer, having sailed with Sea Shepherd to help protect the critically endangered vaquita.
