The career

A life lived
on the water.

Arron's whole life has run through the ocean — yacht crew, scuba instructor, freedive instructor. When he took his first freediving course in 2020, the progression was unusually fast: within a few seasons he was diving to 90 metres.

Now based in Dominica with Blue Element, he is building toward the sport's most symbolic line: 100 metres on a single breath, across FIM, CWTB and CWT.

Beneath the athlete is a committed conservationist — Arron has volunteered with Sea Shepherd, sailing to help protect the critically endangered vaquita, and brings that same purpose to every brand he works with.
