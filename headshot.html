A good overlay photo

  • Portrait (vertical), looking straight at the camera.
  • Turn on your camera's grid: your face, chin to hairline, fills the middle square, with your shoulders in the bottom row. Every athlete is framed the same way on the stream.
  • Plain background and even light. No sunglasses, mask or hat.
  • The original photo from your phone, not a screenshot or a forwarded copy: JPG, PNG or HEIC, up to 10 MB.
