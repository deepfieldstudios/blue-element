For certified divers

Depth is
a system.

Going deep safely is about infrastructure as much as technique. Our platform runs a counter-ballast mainline on 12mm dyneema rope, sonar depth tracking, a dedicated safety chief, in-water safety divers staged through the dive, and surface oxygen on hand.

That means you can focus entirely on the dive — your relaxation, your equalisation, your turn — knowing the system behind you is built to elite-competition standard. AIDA 3 / 30m+ is the minimum to train with us autonomously.
