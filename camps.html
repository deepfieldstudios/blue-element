The camp

One week.
All in.

Mornings filled with freediving on the platform. Afternoon workshops on equalisation and discipline specifics. Evening meditations as the sun drops behind the bay. And one full day away from the line — exploring reefs, waterfalls and hot springs on the Nature Island.

You'll dive with a small group of like-minded souls, coached by world championship athletes, national record holders and 90m+ divers — and leave with new depth, new skills and a professional photo and video pack to prove it.

Every camp includes

A full week, fully supported.

  • Daily yoga & breathwork
  • Mornings of freediving
  • Equalisation & technique workshops
  • Evening meditations
  • Reefs, waterfalls & hot springs day
  • Family-style dinner with local chefs
  • AIDA certifications — all levels
  • Professional photo & video pack

From $725 per week — excludes travel and accommodation. Spots are limited; enquire early for the best rates and accommodation.

Will there be whales?

Our logo is a sperm whale for a reason.

Dominica has an estimated 300 resident sperm whales year-round, with more visiting November through March. We work with licensed local operators to offer whale watching — or permitted swimming with the whales.

Good to know

The practical bits.

Where is it?

Soufrière Bay, Dominica

Dominica sits in the West Indies between Guadeloupe and Martinique — easy connections for European travellers, and direct flights from Miami and Puerto Rico.

How's the food?

Fresh fish & fruit

There's a variety of food options in the village and in the main town of Roseau — expect a lot of fresh fish and fruit, plus our family-style camp dinner with local chefs.

What's the cost?

From $725 a week

Includes the family dinner, photo and video pack, a waterfall day trip, AIDA certifications at all levels, and the full six-day schedule of diving, yoga and breathwork. Travel and accommodation not included.
