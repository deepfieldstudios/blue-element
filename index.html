Why Blue Element

The conditions do half the work.

Soufrière Bay is a natural amphitheatre — deep, sheltered and glass-calm. A custom platform with a 12mm dyneema line, counter-ballast and real-time sonar depth tracking carry the rest.

Jonathan Sunnex

Founder · Depth Chief

“Johnny Deep” — −110m CWT, world depth #3 in 2012/13 and FIM bronze medallist at the 2013 Depth Worlds. Former AIDA Safety Officer and Vertical Blue chief of safety, he has coached five world records and 70+ national and continental records.

Meet the team

Learn from the best in the world.

Blue Element was founded by record-holding freedivers and run by a crew who compete, coach and safety-dive at the highest level. When you train here, the person on the other end of the line has been to the depths you're chasing.

Behind the founders: AIDA & PADI instructor trainers, professional safety and a dedicated medical team.

Harry McCahill

Co-Founder · Managing Director

From a first breath-hold in Hawai'i to three UK CNF records and a world top-8 finish with an 80m dive — the second deepest ever by a UK freediver. AIDA Instructor Trainer, CMAS Worlds competitor and the voice of Vertical Blue 2023.

The annual competition

Where the world comes to go deep.

A flagship AIDA event in Soufrière / Scott's Head Bay — six days of competition diving across CWT, CNF, CWTB and FIM, world-record status, on a platform built for clean, safe deep dives. Organised by Jonathan Sunnex and Harry McCahill with a seasoned international crew.
