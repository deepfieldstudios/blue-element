Our Story

Blue Element exists because two freedivers found the best water on earth — and decided to share it. This is who we are, and what makes the bay so special.

Why we're here

A bay worth
building around.

Blue Element began with a simple realisation: Soufrière Bay in Dominica offers conditions most freedivers only dream of — deep water metres from shore, no current, no waves, and visibility that runs for tens of metres straight down.

We built a training centre and competition around it, run by people who compete and coach at the highest level. The goal has never changed: give every diver, from first breath to world record, the safest and most beautiful place to find their depth.

“We've dived all over the world. We stayed in Dominica because nowhere else lets you go this deep, this calmly, this safely — and we wanted to share it.”
Jonathan Sunnex & Harry McCahill · Founders

The platform & conditions

The conditions do half the work.

Soufrière Bay is a flooded volcanic crater inside a protected marine reserve — a natural deep-water amphitheatre just metres from shore. Our platform turns that geography into a world-class training and competition site.
