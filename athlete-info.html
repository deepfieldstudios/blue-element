Liability release and assumption of risk

I hereby declare that I am, to the best of my knowledge, physically and mentally healthy and capable to compete in the event: Blue Element November 2026.

As far as I am aware, I am not suffering from any chronic diseases of the cardiovascular system, lungs and breathing apparatus, ears, or suffering of any other diseases that would impede my ability to participate in the freediving sport and therefore in this competition. I furthermore state, that I am not currently on any medication that might influence my performance or pose a health risk under strenuous exercise. Should my physical condition be subject to any negative change in the above sense, I will report to the organization committee of the competition and cease any further diving attempts. I have been fully informed of all aspects of freediving through participation in applicable and accepted courses and seminars. I am fully aware of all risks involved in freediving, including hypoxia, shallow water blackout, blood shift and general barotraumas. I will, without fail, follow all instructions of the organization committee, jury, and the safety personal. This applies to any safety relevant instructions given during the actual diving attempts. I am aware that any failure to follow these instructions may lead to immediate exclusion from the competition.

I will not hold Organizers, any members of its board or the organization itself, jury, the safety personal or any other persons associated with it, to be responsible or to be blamed in the case of any accidents or damage to my health stemming from the participation in the competition. I will furthermore not pursue legal steps against them in any of these cases. My participation in this Competition and therefore in any diving attempts is purely at my own risk. The above limitation of responsibility does not extend to criminal negligence or malicious intent. I am aware that my participation in specific diving attempts is purely voluntary.

DISCLAIMER: In the case of any legal problems arising as a result of the participation in the competition and covered in the above text, this document will be used as a legal basis.

I am fully aware of latest version of AIDA Competition Rules.

I am aware that I might be tested for doping & I agree that my doping samples can be used in scientific purposes anonymously.

Liability Release and Assumption of Risk, Version 1.0, © AIDA 2024

Athlete image rights

I, grant the International Association for the Development of Apnea ("AIDA") permission to use my likeness in photographs, and to record my performance, as a sound or audiovisual recording, or on other digital media.

I agree to assign all rights granted herein to AIDA. In particular, I authorize AIDA to exploit on its own behalf or through third parties, whether without charge or otherwise, on any medium, in any format, on any vector, without any restriction whatsoever and without limit of time or place, all the material from my presence at the Blue Element November 2026.
