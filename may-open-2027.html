Safety first

Safety is our number one priority.

Every dive is backed by a full competition setup and an evacuation plan for any outcome. We work with leading industry professionals in freediving medicine and safety.

Competition setup

  • 145m counter-ballasted mainline, Dyneema core for best FIM & CB tension specs
  • Brand-new sonar systems tracking divers deep
  • Professional safety team — World Championship, WR & sled experience
  • Transport boat to and from the platform during competition & training
  • Second counter-ballasted platform to 90m for training dives
  • 2 deco oxygen kits · full face-mask deco kit · 2 medics on standby
  • EZ-PAP CPAP kits for barotrauma & on-site defibrillator
  • Multiple warm-up lines up to 55m · new dry transition area
  • Platform phone & radios linking local team and coast guard

Evacuation plan

  • Evacuation plan for any outcome
  • New hyperbaric chamber at Roseau hospital (opens August 2026)
  • Land, sea and air transport options for every eventuality
  • Small-boat and land-vehicle transfer to hospital in Roseau
  • Coast Guard on alert — air & sea rescue to neighbouring French islands
  • Fast-boat redundancy for channel crossings

Travel

Getting to Dominica.

One of the Caribbean's most beautiful, unspoiled islands. All international visitors arrive via Douglas–Charles Airport (DOM), with regular regional connections through major hubs.

Antigua · ANU Barbados · BGI Guadeloupe · PTP Martinique · FDF Puerto Rico · SJU St. Maarten · SXM Miami · MIA

By ferry

High-speed services connect Dominica with Martinique and Guadeloupe — a good option if you want to combine your trip with other islands.

Travel tips

  • Book flights as early as possible, especially in peak periods
  • Allow extra connection time through regional Caribbean hubs
  • Arrive a day before the event to settle in before the programme starts
  • From the UK & Europe: route via Antigua or Barbados for the easiest connections
  • From North America: direct from Miami, or connect via San Juan, Antigua or Barbados
