The event

Where the world
comes to go deep.

Held each November in Soufrière / Scott's Head Bay, Blue Element is one of the Caribbean's premier freediving competitions — an official AIDA event with full world-record status, organised by Jonathan Sunnex and Harry McCahill with a seasoned international crew of judges, safety and medical professionals.

Athletes from dozens of countries come for the same reason everyone does: water so calm, clear and deep that personal bests and national records fall here year after year.

The disciplines

Four ways to the bottom.

Every athlete announces a target depth the day before. Reach it, return clean, and complete the surface protocol for a white card.

CWT

Constant Weight

Down and up under fin power, following the line without pulling. The classic depth discipline.

CWTB

Constant Weight Bi-fins

The same, on bi-fins only — a discipline of its own with its own records.

CNF

Constant Weight No-Fins

The purest, hardest discipline — propelled by a breaststroke alone, no fins, no line pulls.

FIM

Free Immersion

No fins — the diver pulls hand-over-hand down and back up the line. Elegant and precise.

Past events

A decade in the deep.

From its first edition to today, Blue Element has grown into a fixture on the international freediving calendar — without ever losing the intimacy of a small bay and a tight crew.

Safety & judging

Nothing left to chance.

Every dive is covered by staged in-water safety divers, counter-ballast, sonar tracking, surface oxygen and an on-site medical team — overseen by certified AIDA judges. It's why athletes feel free to push here.
